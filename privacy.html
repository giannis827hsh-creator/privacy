Dusk & Dawn: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Dusk & Dawn ("the app") is made by goodapps12 ("we"). Contact: companyfreeapps680@gmail.com.

The short version: the app works without an account and keeps your data on your phone. If you choose to sign in, we back up and sync your data. We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other apps.

1. What the app stores on your phone

  • Your focus and sleep sessions: when they started, how long they were, whether you finished them, and the optional one-line note you may add.
  • Your settings (appearance, sound mixes, reminder choices, and similar).

This stays on your phone. If you never sign in, none of it is sent to us.

2. If you create an account

You can sign in with an email address and password, or with Google. Then:

  • Account details: your email address (and, for Google, your name as Google provides it) and a random account ID. Passwords are handled only by Google's Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password.
  • Backup and sync: your finished sessions (including notes), settings and sound mixes are copied to our database (Google Cloud Firestore) in a record that only your account can read and change. This lets you keep them when you change phone and use the app on several devices.
  • Pro purchases: if you buy Pro, the purchase is handled by Google Play and our purchase provider RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your account ID and the purchase details (which product, when, whether it is active) so that Pro follows your account. We never receive your payment card details.

3. Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (you can turn them off)

Unless you switch them off in Settings → Privacy → Anonymous usage statistics, the app sends:

  • A few anonymous events (for example "a focus session was started", "a session was finished, 15 to 30 minutes long", "the Pro page was opened") using Firebase Analytics. They never include your notes, your email, or free text.
  • Crash reports using Firebase Crashlytics: what went wrong in the app, and basic technical details about the phone (model, Android version, app version).

Google may also receive a random app-installation ID and general device information (such as country and language) as part of these services. We do not use the advertising ID and the app contains no advertising.

4. Why we use your data

  • To run the app and keep your data safe and in sync (account, backup, sync).
  • To give you the Pro features you paid for.
  • To find and fix problems and to understand which features are used, so we can improve the app (statistics and crash reports).

5. Who processes data for us

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, App Check, Google Play): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (purchases): https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

They process data on our behalf. Data may be processed in countries outside your own, under the protections these providers describe in their policies.

6. How long we keep data

  • Your account data and synced records: until you delete your account.
  • Usage statistics: up to 14 months. Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed for accounting and legal duties.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account. Everything except backup, sync and buying Pro works as a guest.
  • See or take your data: Settings → Export my sessions creates a spreadsheet file (CSV) of every session.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Account → Delete account and all cloud data deletes your account and the copy in our database immediately. Settings → Delete all sessions removes the sessions on your phone (and the cloud copy if you are signed in). You can also ask us by email, or use the page at delete-account.html. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription: cancel that in Google Play.
  • Turn off statistics and crash reports: Settings → Privacy.
  • If you live in the EU/EEA, UK or a similar region you also have the rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to and move your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to us and we will help.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Cloud records are protected so that only your own signed-in account can read or change them. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to keep yours safe.

10. Not medical advice

Dusk & Dawn is a timer and habit tool. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition and is not medical advice. If you have trouble sleeping or concentrating, please talk to a health professional.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where needed, tell you in the app.
