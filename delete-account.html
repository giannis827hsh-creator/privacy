Delete your Dusk & Dawn account and data

This page is for Dusk & Dawn by goodapps12.

Fastest way: inside the app

  1. Open Dusk & Dawn and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account and all cloud data.
  3. Confirm. Your account and the copy of your data in our database are deleted immediately.

Or ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, email companyfreeapps680@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Dusk & Dawn account". We delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, sign-in details, account ID).
  • Your synced sessions (including notes), settings and sound mixes.

What is not deleted, and why

  • The sessions saved on your phone stay on your phone until you delete them (Settings → Delete all sessions) or uninstall the app.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (kept up to 14 months) and crash reports (up to 90 days) are not linked to your email. You can switch them off in Settings → Privacy.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as accounting and legal rules require.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play app: Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Dusk & Dawn Pro → Cancel.
